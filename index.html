THE PROJECT

Art, music, storytelling and professional participation.

Trilema Project by LAEV brings together digital art, music, comics, audiovisual storytelling, physical editions, collectibles, professional collaboration and social and monetary structures.

The project is organized through three complementary instances. This website is the public presentation layer; the GitHub repository is the project's master documentation and development point.

THE THREE INSTANCES

01

Trilema Art Studio

Digital art, comics, characters, visual identity, audiovisual assets, physical editions and collectibles.

02

Trilema Records

Musical art, composition, songwriting, production, recordings, releases and sound-related works.

03

Trilema Institute

Professionals, collaborators, participation, social relationships and monetary structures.

THE THREE COMICS

01

The Bitcoiners Cartel

WHY TRILEMA?

Three as personal identity, cultural reference and technical concept.

The project connects LAEV's personal relationship with the concept of three, the cultural influence associated with the Trilema.com community, and the technical relevance of the trilemma in decentralized network design.

These references form part of the project's identity without implying institutional affiliation, endorsement or ownership by external entities.

COLLABORATION

A project structured to grow through people.

Artists, illustrators, musicians, producers, writers, filmmakers, developers, blockchain and cryptography professionals, researchers and other collaborators can become part of the ecosystem.
