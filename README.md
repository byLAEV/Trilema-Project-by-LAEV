# Trilema Project by LAEV

### A multidisciplinary creative ecosystem inspired by Trilema, digital culture and decentralized technologies.

***

## About the Project

**Trilema Project by LAEV** is a multidisciplinary creative project that brings together digital art, music, comics, audiovisual storytelling, physical editions, collectibles, professional collaboration and social and monetary structures.

The project is developed through three complementary instances:

- **Trilema Art Studio by LAEV** — digital art.
- **Trilema Records by LAEV** — musical art.
- **Trilema Institute by LAEV** — people, professional participation, and the social and monetary dimension of the project.

This repository is the **central presentation and documentation point** for the project.

It is not intended to contain all production materials. Specialized works, productions and technical developments may be documented in separate repositories as the project evolves.

***

# Why Trilema?

The name **Trilema** has several layers of meaning within this project.

The concept of **three** has been a recurring element throughout LAEV's personal experience and environment, creating a personal identification with the term.

The project is also influenced by the culture and ideas associated with the **Trilema.com community**.

At the same time, as a project connected with blockchain and decentralized electronic ecosystems, the term **trilemma** has a direct technical and conceptual relevance to the design of decentralized networks.

These influences meet in **Trilema Project by LAEV**.

The project does not attempt to reproduce any existing work or institution. It develops its own creative ecosystem from these cultural, personal and technological references.

***

# Relation to Blockchain and Decentralized Ecosystems

Trilema Project by LAEV is not presented as a blockchain protocol itself.

Instead, it is a **creative and organizational ecosystem influenced by the ideas, culture and design questions that surround decentralized electronic systems and blockchain technology**.

The project explores how artistic creation, music, storytelling, digital identity, professional participation, social relationships and monetary structures can coexist within contemporary electronic ecosystems.

For this reason, the project is intended to be understandable not only as an art project, but also as an experimental cultural framework related to the broader world of:

- blockchain
- cryptography
- decentralized networks
- electronic money
- digital ownership
- digital identity
- distributed communities
- digital publishing
- audiovisual culture
- online collaboration
