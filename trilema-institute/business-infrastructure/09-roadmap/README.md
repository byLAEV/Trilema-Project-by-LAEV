# 09 — Business Model Roadmap

## Overview

The **Business Model Roadmap** defines the development path for the Trilema creative and economic ecosystem.

It connects the creative works, participating platforms, campaigns, partnerships, content distribution, cryptographic commitments, benefits and economic participation into a progressive business infrastructure.

The roadmap is designed to evolve with the project rather than assuming that every mechanism must exist from the beginning.

---

## Development Path

```text
Foundation
    ↓
Creative Ecosystem
    ↓
Partnerships
    ↓
Campaigns
    ↓
Content & Collectibles
    ↓
Commitment & Revelation Economy
    ↓
Cryptographic Infrastructure
    ↓
Economic Participation
    ↓
Expanded Ecosystem
```

---

## Phase 01 — Foundation

Establish the institutional and creative structure.

### Objectives

- Define **Trilema Institute by LAEV** as the business and coordination infrastructure.
- Define the creative ecosystem.
- Establish the relationship between the three principal creative works.
- Establish documentation standards.
- Define work, issue, edition, version and serial identities.
- Establish the initial repository architecture.

### Primary areas

- Trilema Ecosystem
- Identity & Serials
- Documentation

---

## Phase 02 — Creative Ecosystem

Develop the works and their relationships.

### Objectives

- Develop **The Hanbot Trilema** as the initial narrative work.
- Develop the connected creative works.
- Establish cross-content references.
- Develop stories, songs, editions and collectible content.
- Define how information can be distributed across different works.

### Primary areas

- Content & Collectibles
- Cross-Content Model
- Creative Production

---

## Phase 03 — Partnerships & Platforms

Create structured relationships with companies, platforms and participating organizations.

### Objectives

- Define sponsorship models.
- Define licensing models.
- Define distribution agreements.
- Define exclusive or platform-native content.
- Define co-production structures.
- Define revenue-sharing and royalty models.
- Establish platform-specific participation.

### Principle

Participating platforms may become part of the creative ecosystem through documented content, distribution, campaigns or other agreed forms of participation.

### Primary area

- Partnership Models

---

## Phase 04 — Campaigns

Introduce structured campaigns connecting content with participation and benefits.

### Objectives

- Define campaign identities.
- Define participation conditions.
- Define eligibility.
- Define benefit structures.
- Define redemption conditions.
- Support mass, selective and single participation.
- Support mass, selective, single and first-valid-revelation redemption.

### Primary area

- Campaign Models

---

## Phase 05 — Commitment & Revelation Economy

Introduce the core mechanism for cryptographically committed information and later revelation.

### Core sequence

```text
Creation
→ Participation
→ Commitment
→ Discovery
→ Revelation
→ Verification
→ Benefit
→ Redemption
```

This phase connects the creative content system with a verifiable economic participation layer.

### Objectives

- Commit information before revelation.
- Distribute clues across different works or platforms.
- Enable discovery by participants.
- Verify valid revelations.
- Associate valid revelations with defined benefits.
- Establish redemption rules.

### Primary area

- Commitment & Revelation Economy

---

## Phase 06 — Cryptographic Infrastructure

Formalize the technical mechanisms supporting commitments and verification.

### Objectives

- Define hashing mechanisms.
- Define nonce models.
- Define randomness requirements.
- Define timestamping.
- Define verification protocols.
- Define optional Bitcoin anchoring.
- Separate business rules from cryptographic implementation.

### Principle

Cryptography should support the business model without unnecessarily replacing the creative or commercial logic.

### Primary area

- Cryptographic Infrastructure

---

## Phase 07 — Economic Participation

Expand the economic layer of the ecosystem.

### Objectives

- Define participation economics.
- Define revenue-sharing structures.
- Define royalty models.
- Define campaign benefits.
- Define platform participation economics.
- Define creator and collaborator participation.
- Document economic rules for each campaign or partnership.

### Primary areas

- Economic Participation
- Partnership Models
- Campaign Models

---

## Phase 08 — Expanded Ecosystem

Extend the model across additional platforms, works, campaigns and participating organizations.

### Objectives

- Connect additional creative works.
- Introduce additional platform partnerships.
- Expand cross-content discovery.
- Create more complex campaign structures.
- Support larger participation models.
- Develop additional collectible and reality-linked content.
- Expand the network of participating companies and platforms.

---

## Phase 09 — Future Models

The roadmap remains open to mechanisms that emerge from experimentation.

Possible future areas include:

- advanced cross-content protocols;
- additional verification mechanisms;
- new collectible structures;
- new distribution models;
- decentralized participation systems;
- new forms of platform collaboration;
- additional economic mechanisms.

Future models should be documented as independent concepts before being incorporated into the operational infrastructure.

---

## Business Architecture

The roadmap operates across four connected layers:

| Layer | Function |
|---|---|
| Creative | Stories, comics, music, editions and content |
| Participation | Readers, platforms, partners and collaborators |
| Verification | Commitments, revelations and cryptographic proofs |
| Economic | Benefits, redemption, revenue sharing and royalties |

The layers are connected but remain conceptually separable.
