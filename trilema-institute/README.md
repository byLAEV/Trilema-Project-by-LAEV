# Trilema Institute by LAEV

## Business Infrastructure for the Trilema Ecosystem

Trilema Institute is the business and coordination infrastructure of the **Trilema Ecosystem by LAEV**.

Its purpose is to design, document and coordinate the economic, participatory, partnership, distribution and verification structures that allow creative works, platforms, collaborators and audiences to participate in a connected ecosystem.

Trilema Institute does not replace the creative identity of the individual projects. Instead, it provides the infrastructure through which those projects can interact with partners, platforms, participants and economic mechanisms.

## Creative Ecosystem

The business infrastructure coordinated by Trilema Institute can support:

- **The Hanbot Trilema**
- **The Blockchain OG’s**
- **The Bitcoiners Cartel**
- **Trilema Records**
- **Trilema Art Studio**

These projects remain distinct creative identities while sharing compatible business and participation infrastructure.

## Core Business Model

The Institute develops a reusable framework based on:

**Creation → Participation → Commitment → Discovery → Revelation → Verification → Benefit → Redemption**

This framework is designed to allow different campaigns, publications, music releases, comics, collectibles, collaborations and platform partnerships to use a common underlying structure.

### Key areas

1. **Trilema Ecosystem** — coordination of distributed creation and participation.
2. **Commitment & Revelation Economy** — cryptographic commitments, discovery and revelation mechanisms.
3. **Partnership Models** — sponsorship, licensing, distribution, co-production and revenue-sharing structures.
4. **Campaign Models** — mass, selective and individual participation and redemption models.
5. **Content & Collectibles** — serialised, cross-content and platform-native works.
6. **Identity & Serials** — persistent identifiers for works, issues, editions, versions, campaigns and revelations.
7. **Cryptographic Infrastructure** — hashing, nonces, randomness, timestamping, verification and Bitcoin anchoring.
8. **Campaign Specification** — reusable schemas for participation, commitments, revelations, benefits and redemption.
9. **Roadmap** — progressive development of the Trilema business infrastructure.

## Commitment & Revelation

A central concept of the Institute is that information or benefits may be committed before they are publicly revealed.

A participant may discover information across multiple pieces of content, establish a valid revelation, verify it through the defined protocol, and become eligible for a corresponding benefit or redemption according to the rules of a specific campaign.

This creates a structured relationship between **content, cryptography, participation and economic coordination**.

## Cross-Content Ecosystem

The creative works may contain interconnected information.

A revelation found in one work may require information, context or identifiers found in another. This allows the ecosystem to create deliberate relationships between comics, music, editions, serials, campaigns and other forms of content.

The objective is not merely to publish isolated works, but to create an interconnected body of content that can be explored, verified and collected.

## Platform & Partner Participation

Trilema Institute can coordinate participation by external platforms, companies, creators, sponsors and other partners.

Depending on the project, participation may include:

- exclusive or adapted content;
- co-production;
- distribution;
- sponsorship;
- licensing;
- promotional campaigns;
- platform-native content;
- collectible releases;
- participation benefits;
- revenue-sharing arrangements;
- royalty structures; and
- other commercially defined forms of collaboration.

Specific economic, legal and commercial terms are defined separately for each project or campaign.

## Infrastructure Principle

The Institute follows a simple architectural principle:

> **One concept → one canonical document → multiple references.**

Canonical documents define the underlying model. Other documents and campaigns reference those definitions instead of duplicating them.

This keeps the ecosystem coherent as new creative works, campaigns and participation models are added.

Trilema Institute is intended to function as the **coordination layer for the business infrastructure of the Trilema Ecosystem**.

The creative works create the stories, music, visual art and other content.

The Institute designs and coordinates the infrastructure that allows those works to participate in a broader system of:

**creation · distribution · participation · discovery · verification · benefits · redemption**
