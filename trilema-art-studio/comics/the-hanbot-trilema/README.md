# THE HANBOT TRILEMA
 
## A Comic History by LAEV
 
**The Hanbot Trilema** is a graphic narrative project created and designed by **byLAEV — Lerry Alexander Elizondo Villalobos (LAEV)**.
 
It is a comic, an artistic project and an evolving historical narrative centered on **Hannah W. Wiggins, known as Hanbot, and LAEV**, within a story that connects Bitcoin, electronic money, cryptography, decentralized networks, technology, art and the people who participated in the early culture surrounding these ideas.
 
The project explores a particular intersection between **history, memory, technology, personal relationships, artistic interpretation and the culture of decentralized electronic money**.
 
***
 
# THE STORY
 
At the center of *The Hanbot Trilema* are **Hannah W. Wiggins / Hanbot** and **LAEV**.
 
The story develops around their connection with a broader historical and technological environment that includes **Satoshi Nakamoto, Bitcoin, electronic money, cryptography, blockchain technology, Bitcoin communities and the people who helped shape the culture surrounding decentralized money.**
 
The narrative does not attempt to present history only as a conventional chronological account.
 
Instead, it uses the language of the comic to explore:
 
 
- people;
 
- conversations;
 
- ideas;
 
- historical moments;
 
- technological developments;
 
- relationships;
 
- communities;
 
- conflicts;
 
- discoveries;
 
- memories;
 
- questions;
 
- interpretations;
 
- and possible connections between events.
 

 
The result is intended to be a **comic history**: a narrative work in which historical references, technological concepts, personal stories and artistic interpretation can coexist.
 
***
 
# HANNAH W. WIGGINS — HANBOT
 
Hannah W. Wiggins, known as **Hanbot**, is one of the central figures of the project.
 
Within the story, Hanbot represents a connection between the culture of Bitcoin, professional writing, electronic money and the human side of the history surrounding these technologies.
 
Her presence gives the project one of its principal narrative perspectives.
 
*The Hanbot Trilema* takes its name from this central figure and from the concept of the **trilema** that forms part of the project's identity.

# SATOSHI NAKAMOTO
 
**Satoshi Nakamoto** forms part of the historical and conceptual environment of the story.
 
The project recognizes the importance of the Bitcoin whitepaper, the creation of the Bitcoin network and the Genesis Block as fundamental events in the history of decentralized electronic money.
 
Within *The Hanbot Trilema*, Satoshi is part of the larger historical context in which the characters, technologies and ideas of the story develop.
 
The project may explore the relationship between documented historical information, interpretation, narrative construction and the unanswered questions surrounding the history of Bitcoin.
 
***
 
# THE TRILEMA
 
The word **Trilema** is central to the identity of the project.
 
It operates simultaneously as:
 
 
- the name of the broader creative ecosystem;
 
- a reference to the historical and cultural context surrounding the story;
 
- a structural concept;
 
- and a visual and narrative language.
 

 
The project adopts **1 · 2 · 3** as a recurring structural principle.
 
One can represent:
 
> **One event.**

 
Two can represent:
 
> **Contrast or relationship.**

 
Three can represent:
 
> **Development, sequence or resolution.**

 
This principle becomes part of the visual grammar of the comic.
 
***
 
# THE COMIC AS AN ART PROJECT
 
*The Hanbot Trilema* is designed as a serialized graphic work.
 
Each number may contain:
 
 
- a cover;
 
- contextual material;
 
- narrative pages;
 
- individual scenes;
 
- characters;
 
- visual artwork;
 
- a Future Page;
 
- credits;
 
- and additional material associated with the particular edition.
 

 
The project allows individual pages to exist simultaneously as narrative elements and as independent works of visual art.
 
A page can therefore tell part of the story while also functioning as an artwork in its own right.
 
***
 
# THE FUTURE PAGE
 
One of the distinctive elements of *The Hanbot Trilema* is the **Page of the Future**.
 
This page introduces images, ideas, characters, situations or fragments that may suggest what could happen later in the story.
 
Its purpose is not to guarantee the future narrative.
 
Instead, it introduces:
 
> **possibility, expectation and uncertainty.**

 
The reader is invited to ask:
 
> **What could happen next?**

 
rather than being told that a particular future event is guaranteed.
 
This gives the project room to evolve while maintaining continuity between its different numbers.
 
***
 
# THE READER
 
The reader is not necessarily limited to being a passive observer.
 
The project is designed so that a reader may encounter different layers of the work:
 
### First layer
 
The comic as a story.
 
### Second layer
 
The comic as visual art.
 
### Third layer
 
The comic as a historical and technological object.
 
### Fourth layer
 
The comic as an archival object.
 
### Fifth layer
 
Potential future interactions, discoveries and experiences associated with a particular edition.
 
The depth of the experience therefore depends on how deeply the reader chooses to explore the work.

# A STORY ABOUT PEOPLE AND IDEAS
 
Although technology is an important part of the project, *The Hanbot Trilema* is ultimately a story about people.
 
Technology exists within human relationships.
 
Ideas are created, discussed, challenged and transformed by people.
 
Networks are built by people.
 
Communities are formed by people.
 
And history is remembered through the stories that people preserve.
 
For this reason, the project is interested not only in **what technologies were created**, but also in:
 
> **who was there, what they were thinking about, what they were building, what they were writing, what they believed, what they questioned and how their actions became part of a larger history.**

# A DEVELOPING HISTORY
 
*The Hanbot Trilema* is intentionally conceived as an evolving work.
 
The story may develop through multiple numbers and different forms of media.
 
Some elements may be established through documented historical material.
 
Others may be presented as artistic interpretation, narrative construction, questions or possibilities.
 
The project therefore leaves room for discovery.
 
A future publication may add context to something previously seen.
 
A character introduced briefly may later become important.
 
An apparently insignificant detail may acquire another meaning.
 
A question may remain unanswered.
 
And a reader may discover connections between different parts of the work.
 
***
 
# THE PURPOSE OF THE PROJECT
 
The purpose of *The Hanbot Trilema* is to create a graphic work capable of preserving and exploring a particular story and the technological culture surrounding it.
 
It seeks to combine:
 
> **HISTORY + ART + TECHNOLOGY + PEOPLE + MEMORY**

 
into a serialized narrative.
 
The project is an attempt to create a form of comic that can function simultaneously as:
 
**a story,**
 
**an artwork,**
 
**a historical narrative,**
 
**a cultural document,**
 
and **an evolving creative project.**
